Forecasting economic series from FRED

ARIMA and gradient-boosted models on live Federal Reserve data, side by side.

Zain Hameed · 2025

Pick a series (energy consumption, retail sales or GDP), adjust the model parameters, and compare how a classical time-series model and a machine-learning model forecast it, with intervals. The data comes straight from the St. Louis Fed's FRED API, so the forecast moves when the data does.

How it works

  • Live series from the FRED API; pandas and NumPy for preparation.
  • ARIMA from statsmodels and XGBoost for the machine-learning model, with adjustable parameters.
  • Confidence intervals on every forecast and a direct comparison of the two methods.
  • Plotly for the interactive charts.
