Zain Hameed

I build models of how operations actually run, and use econometrics to tell which changes worked.

For four years I've done that from the inside, as the analyst operators call when a number doesn't make sense: first on grain origination, processing and freight at ADM, then on Medicare primary care at Oak Street Health. The work is usually the same shape. Find out what the business means by its own words, write that down as data models someone can test, and then answer the question that started it.

I studied economics at the University of Michigan. I'm most interested in companies that make physical things, and in the state capacity and industrial base those things depend on.
